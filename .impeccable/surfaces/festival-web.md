# Festival Web Surface

- Mode: Operate.
- Scope: student Home, Map, Stamps, Vouchers, Reviews, MY and existing operator tools.
- Approved direction: Bright Festival Pass, white/mint with green ink stamps.

## Direction Contract

THESIS: Separate finding, visiting, rating and claiming in a bright festival pass. Do not blur a visit count, a completed rating and an issued reward into one status.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, deep green ink, mint actions, restrained blue information and yellow claimable rewards. Compact Korean typography, flat sections, double-ring stamps and labeled icons carry the identity.

STORY: Personal progress leads to the map and booth detail, then a verified visit and rating, an ink stamp, and the appropriate voucher. Pending written reviews remain reachable. Operators navigate existing tasks with protected confirmations.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact festival header, personal record and illustrated map action lead into pending reviews. The map exposes search, floor controls and the first list row. Stamps expose the count and wallet above a scrollable path. Operator screens begin with actual event status and task groups.

FORM: Integrate the user-approved festival-pass structure into the existing web app. Preserve real floorplan geometry, server boundaries and task behavior; do not transplant mock locations or unsupported management actions.

## Preserved Boundaries

- Preserve authentication, NFC claims, real-name review checks, repeated three-stamp reward contract, Korean IME, browser history and update-only single-event operations.
- Keep actual school geometry and known locations. Do not invent main/annex assignments or make synthetic mock coordinates operational facts.
- Do not introduce unimplemented notice or emergency server actions, review edits unsupported by the API, or new DB changes.
- Keep visit completion independent from paused-booth state and voucher issuance.
